sanasay
aicu a sanasay aya tjen mavan a kedri a kadjunangan a pinimaza i vecekadan i tua ljavek. a i pasakaledep mavan a tayuan. a i pasanavalj avan a pongso no tao . kasicuayan, qadjav tu nu i 1800 a patje 1900 a cavilj, aicu a kadjunangan a pinapungadan nua sebaljak tua Sama-Sana (sivecik uta tua Samasana).
