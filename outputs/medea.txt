medea
a tja kineljang aicu a ti medea i tua milimilingan nua segeris (Greece) mavan aljak a vavayan nua mazangilj nua secolchis (Colchis) i aites (Aeëtes), a vuvu a vavayan nua cemas nua qadav i hilius (Helios). a kemasi sinansenay a tjiuguni (Theogony) ni hisiud (Hesiod) a tjevuta ti medea a kiseljang tjai djasun (Jason) kati argunauc (Argonauts) namatazua i pasusangas tua 2700 a cavilj (700 BCE) a vinecikan tua milimilingan.
