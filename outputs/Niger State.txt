Niger State
Niger namu a Nigeria sa na North Central i. Liyanan a tanedju, tanedju Minna a makakaulangan. Nukaljini a Bida, Kontagora, tamaya Suleja. Sangka 1976, tamavahec a North-Western a nangamu a Niger tamaya Sokoto. Maljangek tu naluzen, Nupe, Gbagyi, Kamuku, Kambari, tamaya Hausa a kangulu, asi Nupe a tamasudumangan a teremi. [ 1 ]
