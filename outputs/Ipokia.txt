Ipokia
Ipokia so metropolina ka puziya so Anago. So kavaisan na Ipokia simakatiana ma dakal a puruma na Oyo empire ya dina a masau ditutu ka mapuliman a Yorubaland ka 19th century. Katakitia a kinaingin na Ipokia ma 13th siso 14th century ya sangngaling tua Oramiyan, 6th Ooni na Ife, inenga ka Badagry tamaya nimu dediyu so Ipokia na ma'iru a "Opo ko ara e sile" tamaya kaisa so Ifa Oracle. Ipokia so magabay ka Local Government ka Ogun State ma 1996 tamaya yikikoma ya Nigeria ka Republic of Benin.
